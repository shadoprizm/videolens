VideoLens Support

For help with a VideoLens account, saved report, browser extension, or ChatGPT connection, email astrawebdevservices@gmail.com. Include the feature you were using and what happened. Do not send API keys, passwords, or private video content.

For ChatGPT access, sign in to the same VideoLens account that holds your cloud-saved reports. The plugin reads only reports you chose to save to your cloud library. You can remove connected app access from your account page.
