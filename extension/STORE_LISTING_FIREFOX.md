# Firefox Add-ons listing — English (US)

Verified public description for 0.8.4 on October 29, 2026. Paste plain text into the AMO description editor; do not copy the API-rendered outgoing-link HTML.

```text
VideoLens turns the video already open in Firefox—or a local video file—into a polished written report in Firefox's native sidebar. Findings cite timestamped evidence, so you can check their source and context.

Use VideoLens for detailed reports, key insights, tutorials, interviews and podcasts, meeting notes, bug reports, UX reviews, product demos, content reviews, and privacy scans.

Features:
• Analyze YouTube, compatible HTML5 video players, and local MP4, WebM, or MOV files
• Timestamped findings grounded in sampled frames and available captions or transcription
• Independent non-speech sound observations for supported Managed local files after accepting the updated processing notice
• Follow-up questions against the analyzed timeline
• Private local report library with search, backup, restore, reopen, and delete controls
• Export self-contained HTML, print-ready PDF, Markdown, JSON, or copied text
• Reports in English, Simplified or Traditional Chinese, Latin American Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Romanian, or Hindi

Two analysis modes:

Private mode uses your own OpenAI API key. The key stays in Firefox's local extension storage, and the selected frames, audio or captions, page title, prompt, and follow-up questions go directly from Firefox to OpenAI. VideoLens does not receive that analysis content.

Optional Pro mode removes API-key setup. Analysis content is sent through VideoLens and Vercel AI Gateway to OpenAI. After the updated processing notice is accepted, optimized general reports also use Baseten serving Z.ai GLM for final review, with OpenAI as a fallback. Speech transcription and other report types use OpenAI. Local-file sound analysis additionally sends short audio windows to DeepInfra serving Xiaomi MiMo after accepting the updated notice. A free account includes three managed starter reports; paid plans provide additional managed reports. Saving a completed report to the cloud library is optional and off by default.

Completed reports and follow-up answers are saved locally on your device unless you explicitly enable cloud saving in Pro mode. VideoLens does not sell your data or use analysis content for advertising.

Important limits: Non-speech sound analysis is available for supported Managed local files up to 20 minutes. Browser-tab captions and Private mode do not analyze non-speech sounds. Acoustic observations are provisional and are kept separate from visual and spoken evidence. DRM-protected players such as Netflix and Disney+ cannot be captured. Live streams are unsupported. Cross-origin video protections on some sites may prevent frame capture.
```
