Data coverage

This dashboard covers registered accounts and retained managed-report and cloud-library records. Private / BYOK scans stay on the device unless the user uploads a report to their cloud library. Anonymous installs and hosted-app activity are not linked to these accounts. Titles and modes appear only when reported by the client; older or interrupted scans may have no details. Deleted reports are excluded. Paid Pro means an active Stripe-linked subscription, not confirmed payment revenue. Complimentary Pro is counted separately.
